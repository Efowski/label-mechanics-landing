03 / A WORKING PRINCIPLE

You might not need more marketing.

Label Mechanics isn't being built to convince every label to spend more. Sometimes a Bandcamp announcement, a few posts and direct contact may be enough.

The useful question is knowing when it is enough — and what your own release history can teach you before you spend the next euro or the next evening on promotion.
